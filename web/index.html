GmG · Guess My Genome

Sketch-based tools for fungal genomes and reads

Both run in your browser. Your own files are sketched in the tab and never uploaded.

GmG: classify genomes or reads

Phylum to species from a FracMinHash sketch, compared with a reference index.

popsketch: audit a set of read files

Low coverage, wrong size, a second organism, unrelated samples; then a PCA, clusters and a tree. Use your own FASTQ files, or fetch the first reads of SRA/ENA runs by accession or taxon.
