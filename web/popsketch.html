Or open a sketch bundle

A bundle is a set of sketches made earlier on a computer or cluster with popsketch sketch and packed with scripts/popsketch_bundle.py: a few MB per sample instead of the reads. Open one from your disk or from a link (the server must allow cross-origin requests). The page also reads ?data=URL.

Labels from ENA (optional)

If your files are named by run accession (SRR1713175_1.fastq.gz), fetch country, study, host, strain and species for them from ENA and use those as labels. Only the accessions are sent to ENA; your reads stay here.
